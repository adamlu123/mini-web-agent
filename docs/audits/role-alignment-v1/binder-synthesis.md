# Binder/Critic alignment synthesis

## Bottom line

Binder/Critic banked score when it converted task-visible facts into exact,
evaluator-facing contracts and Actor carried them into persistent state. Broad
process prose, quality claims, and generic checklists did not bank score.

## Where alignment banked score

- **013:** exact optimum/amount allocation; full score.
- **015:** corrected measurements and downstream calculations; near-full score.
- **029:** exact 61-marker vector; near-full score despite missing live QA.
- **017:** correct stop set/walking route banked partial credit.
- **054:** exact output path/shape banked export credit.
- **075:** server/content contract banked partial credit while visuals failed.

## Evaluator preferences captured

- exact paths, filenames, keys, casing, punctuation, numeric serialization,
  hashes, marker vectors, and cardinality;
- native editable/parser-visible structure;
- canonical initialized app/profile/session;
- exhaustive finite sets and forbidden extras;
- source/baseline preservation;
- post-save/reload readback;
- actual visual geometry where task-visible;
- hard gates and weighted partials.

## Remaining task-visible gaps

- missing artifacts and premature submission;
- wrong final surface or no readback;
- incomplete inventories;
- missing native serialized fields;
- visible geometry defects;
- unperformed live QA;
- domain capability limitations.

## Hidden or conflicting gaps

- evaluator-only dependencies/temp files;
- hard-coded alternate database paths;
- hidden ordering or exact gold strings;
- narrow evaluator proxies;
- evaluator undercoverage;
- infrastructure hard gates.

## General principles

1. Alignment is the intersection of task semantics, evaluator observables, and
   executable evidence.
2. Exact facts require unique visible support.
3. Unresolved probes remain unresolved.
4. Persistent native state/readback outranks screenshots or command success.
5. Finite sets require exhaustive membership and no-extra semantics.
6. Score and task correctness are not equivalent.
7. More Binder steps, checklists, or a fresh Verifier do not create alignment.
